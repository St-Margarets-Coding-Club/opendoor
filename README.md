# 😸 OpenDoor

An animal adoption website [useopendoor.com](https://opendoor-pink.vercel.app/) built by high schoolers from Hong Kong, with aesthetic UI/UX and a social media approach where you could only view one profile at a time. Our database which stores the animal profiles are layered public data from [Hong Kong Animal Adoption Database](https://hkaad.siuyeong.com/) which has animals from major animal shelters.

## 🚀 Features

- Aesthetic user interface
- Sourced from major animal shelters
- Simple and easy to use
 
## 📍 The Initiative

Animal adoption rates is very low in Hong Kong and there's a couple reasons for that, like low public awareness, inefficient animal shelters and insufficient support from the Government.

We believe that a modern approach with a different way to thinking would be able to increase the adoption rates in Hong Kong, which is by building software and doing sales.

If you want to learn more on our building process and sales approach, please click the links below.

- [DESIGN.md](https://github.com/St-Margarets-Coding-Club/opendoor/blob/main/ENGINEERING.md) Documents our code

## 🐾 The Goals

Initiatives are abstract. Therefore, we outlined the goals we want to achieve.

1. Help as many animals as possible to find a place they call home
2. Increase the public, animal shelters and the Government awareness on animal adoptions
3. Document the whole process for anyone to replicate it in their own city
